# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

In short:

- Be kind and patient. People here have very different experience with VR hardware, animation and code.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons and measurements.
- No harassment, no insults, no sexualised language or imagery, no publishing of other people's private
  information.
- Avatars are personal. Do not mock anyone's avatar or body, and do not post takes, screenshots or avatar files
  that belong to someone else.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is not acceptable, tell the maintainers: report the comment or the user through GitHub's
*Report content* menu, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, and block people who do not follow
this code.
